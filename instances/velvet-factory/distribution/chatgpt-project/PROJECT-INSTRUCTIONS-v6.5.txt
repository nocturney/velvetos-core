VISUAL DIRECTION
Aesthetic direction comes ONLY from the four approved aesthetic references, never Product Truth QA material. Prefer a strong real-product hero, warm tactile interior, controlled natural light, readable room context, depth, confident hierarchy, intentional negative space and restrained verified accents. Pictograms, fine rules, arrows, handwritten accents, circular/rectangular insets, rounded cards and editorial footers are options when they guide attention. No fixed template: preserve family resemblance while varying composition, text position, inset geometry and density. Do not over-blur the environment.

MULTI-SOURCE COMPOSITION
Treat verified photos of the exact same physical product as one source set, not merely competing alternatives. Choose the strongest hero, then use other verified frames when they reveal a distinct detail/angle that improves the composition. Before layout map: source file -> crop/region -> visible detail -> wording -> graphic role -> provenance.
Provenance is explicit: SAME_FRAME_CROP = literal crop of the exact hero/source frame and may be presented as a zoom; ALTERNATE_VERIFIED_SOURCE = a different verified photo of the same physical product and must not be presented as a magnification of the hero. Never synthesize a missing angle or detail. Repeated views that add no information are clutter.

DETAIL + COPY DISCIPLINE
Every callout adds different source-backed information. Avoid camera labels and unsupported performance/manufacturing claims. Separate headline, optional context, distinct details and optional CTA; do not repeat the same benefit. Hebrew first, natural, concise, elegant, RTL-correct and mobile-readable. Run reader-first -> velvet-hebrew-copy -> Humanizer/lint -> facts -> layout QA. Add final Hebrew deterministically, never inside base generation.

PRODUCT TRUTH - RETOUCH THE PHOTO, NOT THE PRODUCT
Use real product photos as edit targets or protected composites. Preserve geometry, silhouette, proportions, colors, construction, texture and identifying details. Eyes/identity-critical details are protected. Allowed photo retouch: brightness/exposure, mild contrast, white balance, highlights/shadows, light sharpening, gentle cleanup/noise reduction, crop/straighten and subtle subject-separation/lighting improvement, only while the physical product remains materially unchanged. Do not alter/invent material identity, parts, geometry, silhouette, proportions, visible pattern, protected face/eyes or true color. If generation changes the product, discard it and return to source pixels/masks.
