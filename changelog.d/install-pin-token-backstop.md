- 2026-10-18 - **Install-pin token backstop:** `scripts/check-install-pins.py` splits every workflow `run:` block with `shlex`, including nested `-c` and `eval` strings. A word whose basename is pip, pip3*, uv, uvx, pipx, npm, npx, pnpm, yarn, or bun, or a `python*` followed by `-m pip` / `-mpip`, must be a pinned or lock install. Otherwise the step is unclassified. That covers `if`/`for`, `{ }`, `!`, `timeout`, `nohup`, `eval`, `xargs`, and `bash -lc`. A `shlex` failure refuses the step. Turning the token gate off lets those prefixes through, and a bare `pip install` stays caught. `INSTALL_SPEC_ALLOWLIST` stays empty. `security_admission` stays RED.
