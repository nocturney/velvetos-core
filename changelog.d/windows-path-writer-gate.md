Before, an extra step that downloaded a tool and ran `Add-Content -Path $env:GITHUB_PATH` passed both sensors, before or after the ffmpeg step. The self-test inserts 19 writer steps before and after the ffmpeg step, plus a second same-name ffmpeg step, and checks each is caught. A benign extra download step without a PATH write still passes. The workflow itself is unchanged.
