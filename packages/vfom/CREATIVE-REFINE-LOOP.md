# Velvet creative refine loop — provider-neutral

Status: **MANDATORY METHOD INSIDE THE EXISTING VISUAL FOUNDRY**  
Source inspiration: MIT-licensed `scenario-labs/skills` v0.48.0.  
Authority remains Product Truth, Visual OS, Content Contract, Brand Guardian, Visible Text
and exact-final publication evidence. External publication remains `policy_id: instagram.publish`;
this refine loop never authorizes the effect by itself.

This is not a Scenario workflow and does not require Scenario MCP, credits, storage or
model access.

## 0. Stage 6B authority and escalation boundary

The Creative Manifest records the working candidate, rubric, evidence, repairs and projections; it is not an approval database. Creative Craft 2.0 and its routed specialists are an authoring/quality system, not a new policy layer. Product Truth is higher authority than aesthetic references, score, taste, model output or provider preference.

`produce → critique → targeted refine → recheck` is an internal loop. Ordinary aesthetic choices and ordinary quality failures are resolved by the office with the smallest evidence-backed repair; do not ask the owner which hook, crop, cover, grade, composition or provider they “prefer”. Escalate only the explicit exception-only human surface in `FOUNDRY.json` / the active Instance, or a hard blocker after bounded failover.

## 1. Product-shot baseline

For a real Velvet Factory product, the product is never regenerated from text.

Before the first edit:

1. choose the real source frame/source set;
2. write a fidelity inventory for silhouette, proportions, parts/openings, surface identity,
   color/finish and identity-critical details;
3. lock the source crop and protected regions;
4. write the creative target separately: scene, surface, light, depth, hierarchy and
   negative space;
5. keep approved STYLE references comparison-only. They are not product truth and are not
   image-conditioning inputs in the Native Product Edit route.

A scene prompt/brief must subordinate the world to the product. Shadows/contact, perspective
and surface grounding belong to the scene; product geometry does not.

## 2. Rubric before the first attempt

Write checkable pass/fail criteria **before** generating/editing. Use observable conditions,
not taste words.

The rubric must include:

- Product Truth / source identity;
- required scene/composition behavior;
- reference-alignment dimensions;
- mobile readability / negative space when text will be added;
- forbidden claims/branding/text;
- final materialization requirement when deterministic overlays are expected.

Do not redefine the rubric after seeing a weak output.

## 3. Baseline + delta

Every retry names the invariant baseline and one bounded delta.

Invariant baseline includes source identity, protected details, accepted scene/master
properties and every already-passing criterion. The delta is only the failed scope.

Never use a drifted product render as the new identity baseline. Identity repair returns to
the verified source.

## 4. Diagnose before repairing

Route a failed criterion to the smallest repair that can actually fix it:

| Failure | Route |
|---|---|
| product/identity drift | targeted source-grounded repair; if it cannot be restored reliably, discard the edited subject and use the canonical source-preserving fallback |
| one local visual defect | localized edit on that region; keep the rest of the accepted candidate |
| global composition/scene failure | creative retry from the approved identity baseline |
| crop/grade/finish only | deterministic edit, not a full reroll |
| repeated model/tool failure | switch method/provider only after the bounded repair policy is exhausted |
| unsupported text/logo/claim | remove it; add exact verified text/logo deterministically after MASTER |

Change one variable per repair round whenever practical. A retry that simultaneously swaps
provider, prompt, references and composition cannot teach the system why it improved.

The Revision 6.6.9 bounded limits remain authoritative: at most two targeted identity
repairs and at most two creative retries before the defined fallback path.

## 5. Review the unit you are willing to reject

Inspect the exact candidate and mobile view. For a composite/carousel, inspect individual
critical units as well as the whole.

AI quality scoring may be a hint, never proof of:

- product identity;
- physically possible structure;
- reference fidelity;
- factual claims;
- source provenance.

Product Identity Gate and Creative Gate remain separate.

## 6. Freeze MASTER, then derive

Once identity and creative quality both pass, freeze the real file-backed MASTER.

Derivatives are **derivations**, not independent re-generation:

1. same ratio, different pixels -> deterministic resize;
2. tighter ratio with expendable edges -> crop only if subject/safe areas survive;
3. more canvas -> expand/uncrop while preserving the MASTER;
4. different ratio needing recomposition -> source/master-grounded reframe;
5. far ratio/new hierarchy -> deliberate derivative composition with MASTER/source
   provenance, followed by the same gates.

Reframe/expand before final finishing passes. Add Hebrew, logo, CTA, legal text and other
letter-perfect overlays last, separately per final canvas. The existing deterministic RTL
finalizer and product guard remain authoritative.

## 7. Video / ad / storyboard adaptation

The useful upstream video patterns are adopted without the Scenario backend:

- script/brief first, then shot list/storyboard;
- one identity/source pack across shots;
- generate/build only the shots that are actually missing;
- preserve source-backed product identity in every shot;
- assemble, caption and resize through the existing VelvetOS video/HyperFrames/FFmpeg
  stack;
- captions/CTA/brand text are deterministic final layers;
- final format derivatives come from the approved master edit, not from unrelated rerolls.

UGC/avatar/fan-cam/model-specific generation is not enabled merely because the upstream
repository documents it.

## 8. Provider comparison

When a provider/model decision is genuinely unresolved, compare candidates on the **same**
source, brief, constraints and acceptance rubric. Record:

- identity/reference pass/fail;
- creative pass/fail;
- materializable output;
- latency/cost only from observed or approved data;
- unresolved defects.

Do not rank providers using marketing claims and do not create a permanent provider lock
from one bake-off.

## Completion rule

A successful loop ends with the strongest candidate that passed the existing gates and is
materialized into the canonical workspace. It does not end merely because a generation
job returned successfully.
