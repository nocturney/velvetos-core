The cable-holder source is a tighter first experiment than a larger organizer: clear desk utility, no hardware in the model, Public Domain / Commercial Use, and the source provides a concrete reference print of 9 g / 56 min on a Prusa MK3S. That reference is **not** Velvet production data.

**Confidence:** high for source/license/reference-print facts; medium for operational fit; low for local demand until Velvet tests it.

### 2. Desk utility is a family signal, not a single-model bet

Fresh Printables pages plus the desk index repeatedly surface cable management, sticky-note/pen holders, under-desk storage and organizers. This supports testing a **small functional desk family** rather than another decorative-only item. It does not prove Israeli or Sderot sales demand.

**Velvet action:** keep the test ladder narrow: cable holder first, sticky-note holder second. Do not fill all five shelf slots from marketplace popularity. One local print + photo + real inquiry signal should precede expansion.

**Confidence:** medium for category persistence; low for local demand.

### 3. Slicer workflow: benchmark the idea, not the preview

PrusaSlicer 3.0 Preview's multi-bed project model can reduce context switching when preparing several jobs, but Prusa labels the build experimental and not recommended for production. The studio Mac currently has `Snapmaker Orca.app` version **2.3.5** installed, verified locally on 2026-09-13.

**Velvet action:** do not switch slicers. If batch preparation becomes a measured bottleneck, compare current Snapmaker Orca workflow against the multi-bed concept using representative jobs and operator-time/error counts. Adopt only a proven workflow improvement compatible with the actual fleet.

**Confidence:** high for Prusa capability/preview status and local installed-version observation; medium for potential Velvet benefit.
