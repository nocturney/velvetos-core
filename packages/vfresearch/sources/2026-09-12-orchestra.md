### 1. Cable-management remains a credible repeatable-product direction
Current Etsy listings continue to show active demand and buyer feedback for compact 3D-printed cable organizers, cord winders, under-desk holders and related desk accessories. Recent examples include a cable-tidy listing updated/listed in August 2026 with verified buyer reviews, and a shop specializing in desk/cable gear with hundreds of sales.

### 2. Practical desk accessories are stronger candidates than decorative-only tests
Recent marketplace examples cluster around cable holders, organizers, hooks and compact workspace accessories. These products are small, easy to demonstrate in short-form content and often compatible with support-free or low-complexity FDM geometry.

Implication for Velvet: prioritize one small support-free cable holder / desk organizer candidate for internal print-time, grams, finish quality and licensing checks before adding any catalog item or price.

### 3. Customization is a useful differentiator, but license/fit constraints matter
Buyer feedback on current cable-management listings explicitly values customization and fit. Some listings also note that cable/device fit can be very specific.

Implication for Velvet: if testing this family, define one base model plus a limited set of fit variants rather than open-ended customization. Record commercial-use license before production.
