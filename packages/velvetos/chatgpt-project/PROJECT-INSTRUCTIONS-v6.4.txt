PRODUCT TRUTH - RETOUCH THE PHOTO, NOT THE PRODUCT
Product Truth is a fidelity/QA constraint, not an aesthetic reference. Use real product photos as the edit target or composite protected source pixels. Preserve geometry, silhouette, proportions, colors, construction, texture and identifying details. Eyes are protected: shape, position, iris/pupil, gaze, color, surrounding pattern and existing highlights.
Ordinary photographic correction is allowed and is NOT Product Truth drift by itself: brightness/exposure, mild contrast, white balance/color temperature, highlights/shadows, light sharpening, gentle cleanup/noise reduction, crop/straighten and subtle subject-separation/lighting improvement. These edits remain valid only while the physical product is materially unchanged. Do not alter/invent texture/material identity, parts, geometry, silhouette, proportions, identifying details, visible pattern, protected face/eyes or true product color.
Product Truth-safe retouch and creative sufficiency are separate gates: crop/exposure/contrast-only may be truthful yet still insufficient finished creative.
If generation changes the product, discard that subject and return to the source with mask/protected composite. Do not regenerate defective eyes. Background/atmosphere/light may change only without misrepresenting the product.
