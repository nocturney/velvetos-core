VELVET FACTORY — PRODUCT TRUTH GUIDE v1
Status: QA / FIDELITY LAW ONLY · NOT A STYLE REFERENCE

PURPOSE
Protect the real photographed product without contaminating the aesthetic direction. This file may guide source locking, masks, crop provenance and final QA. It must not be used as a moodboard, style image, generation reference or composition template.

SOURCE OF PHYSICAL TRUTH
The actual verified photograph(s) of the current product are the only source for geometry, silhouette, proportions, parts, openings, surface markings, color, material appearance, construction and identifying details.

ALLOWED PHOTO RETOUCH
Brightness/exposure; mild contrast; white balance/color temperature; highlights/shadows; light sharpening; gentle cleanup/noise reduction; crop/straighten; subtle subject-separation or lighting improvement. These are photo retouch, not synthetic subject change, only while the physical product remains materially unchanged.

FORBIDDEN PRODUCT CHANGE
Do not alter or invent texture/material identity, parts/elements, geometry, silhouette, proportions, identifying details, visible surface pattern, protected face/eyes or true product color.

PROTECTED DETAILS
Identity-critical details must match source exactly. For the owl example this includes eye shape/size/spacing, iris/pupil pattern and color, gaze, eyelids, surrounding pattern/highlights, beak, ear tufts, feather markings, claws and base geometry/texture. Apply the same principle to analogous details on other products.

DETAIL CARDS
If a detail card is presented as a zoom/magnification of the hero, it is a literal deterministic crop of that exact hero/source frame. No independently generated subject/background, relighting, recoloring or separate retouch inside the crop.

GENERATION SEPARATION
Do not feed Product Truth QA boards or teaching sheets into image generation/style conditioning. Use the approved aesthetic references for look and feel; use source pixels + this text for truth.

FAILURE RESPONSE
If a generative edit changes the product, discard that subject and return to source pixels with a protected mask/cutout or deterministic composite. Do not repeatedly regenerate defective eyes/identity details.

FINAL QA
Compare exact final vs source at full and mobile size. Verify source match, protected details, allowed-retouch bounds, same-frame crop provenance, safe margins, truthful color/material, and absence of invented parts/claims.
