Treat verified photos of the exact same physical product as one source set. Choose the strongest hero; use other verified frames when they reveal a distinct useful detail/angle. Map source file -> crop/region -> visible detail -> wording -> graphic role -> provenance.
SAME_FRAME_CROP = literal crop of exact hero/source frame and may be shown as zoom. ALTERNATE_VERIFIED_SOURCE = different verified photo of the same physical product and must not masquerade as hero magnification. Never synthesize a missing angle/detail. Repetitive views are clutter.

PRODUCT TRUTH - RETOUCH THE PHOTO, NOT THE PRODUCT
Use real product photos as edit targets/protected composites. Preserve geometry, silhouette, proportions, colors, construction, texture and identifying details; eyes/identity-critical details are protected. Allowed retouch: exposure, mild contrast, white balance, highlights/shadows, light sharpening, gentle cleanup/noise reduction, crop/straighten and subtle subject separation, only while the physical product stays materially unchanged. Do not invent/change material identity, parts, geometry, silhouette, proportions, visible pattern, protected face/eyes or true color. If generation changes the product, discard it and return to source pixels/masks.
