# Creative Craft — local execution guide

Scope: routed multi-tool creative/DCC execution.

- Route through `CreativeCraftRouter.py` and `creative-craft-registry.json`; load `skill/SKILL.md` only when the request actually needs Creative Craft.
- Pick the minimum accepted tool/specialist for the operation; do not activate the full DCC warehouse.
- Preserve typed capability/readiness surfaces and compatibility gates. Do not bypass a failed adapter by inventing execution.
- Tool output is artifact evidence, not policy authorization.
- Fabrication/production requests also obey `packages/vfprod/AGENTS.md`; public creative obeys `packages/vfom/AGENTS.md`.
- No physical printer action, spend decision or external publication authority originates here.
Policy routing reference: `policy_id: project.request.preflight` is router-only; Creative Craft output is evidence, not external-effect authority.
