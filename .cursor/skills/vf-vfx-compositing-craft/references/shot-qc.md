# VFX shot QC

- Review at playback speed, then inspect worst/stress frames at useful zoom.
- Include first/middle/last, fastest motion, largest occlusion, hardest key edge and strongest contrast boundary when applicable.
- Check lens geometry, matte integrity, track stability, alpha/premultiplication, pass reconstruction and integration as separate dimensions.
- Look for grain discontinuity, edge halos, sharpness mismatch, defocus mismatch, motion-blur mismatch, slipping tracks, matte chatter and temporal color pops.
- Re-open/read back the exact output and verify frame range/metadata plus representative frame hashes when the workflow records them.
- A successful render is not proof of integration quality, editorial approval or final color approval.
