# Integration and finishing

- Match the plate image system, not an abstract notion of realism.
- Check perspective, contact, shadow, exposure relationship, color temperature, black/white behavior, atmospheric depth, sharpness, grain/noise, defocus and motion blur.
- Perform broad integration before local edge polish.
- Match grain/noise structure after major resampling and defocus decisions so it is not blurred or sharpened inconsistently.
- Use edge treatment that reflects the source optics and motion rather than a universal blur/erode recipe.
- Keep creative grade and final shot matching under post-production ownership after VFX integration is technically coherent.
