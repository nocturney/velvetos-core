# Anatomy, likeness and sculpt QA

Use for human/creature/stylized sculpt review. Public/commercial sculpt training informs these concepts only; no course media or proprietary diagrams are bundled.

- Lock primary gesture, silhouette, proportion and large plane changes before chasing pores or micro-detail.
- Compare landmark relationships and negative spaces from multiple views; a convincing single angle does not prove a coherent volume.
- Separate anatomical plausibility from likeness. Likeness depends on specific proportion, plane, rhythm and asymmetry evidence from the reference subject.
- Preserve intentional asymmetry while distinguishing it from accidental drift.
- Check form under simple neutral lighting and rotating views so material detail cannot hide weak structure.
- For deformation targets, verify joint/volume behavior independently of the high-resolution sculpt.
- For stylized work, identify which anatomical relationships are deliberately exaggerated and keep those rules consistent.
- Do not claim identity/likeness from memory or generic anatomy; require appropriate reference evidence.
