# Orientation and supports

- Identify load direction, functional surfaces, mating faces, cosmetic faces and critical dimensions before orienting.
- Favor layer orientation that supports the required mechanical behavior; do not assume isotropic strength.
- Keep critical mating surfaces away from support contact when that materially affects finish/accuracy.
- Use the least support that still makes the print reliable; avoid support settings chosen only to reduce estimated time.
- Inspect bridges, overhangs, thin walls, islands and first-layer contact in the slicer preview.
- For multi-part jobs, consider per-part failure isolation, cooling interaction and collision constraints of the actual printer mode.
- Do not hardcode one support-angle threshold for all geometry/material/printer combinations.
