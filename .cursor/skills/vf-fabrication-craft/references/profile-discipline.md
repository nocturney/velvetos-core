# Profile discipline

- Keep vendor/default profiles identifiable and create derived profiles for intentional changes.
- Record printer, nozzle, filament/material, process and slicer version with any tuned profile.
- Change one major variable at a time during diagnosis when practical.
- Do not transfer a tuned profile to a materially different printer/nozzle/material without validation.
- Distinguish geometry compensation from extrusion compensation and from motion tuning.
- Preserve rollback to the last known-good profile.
- After slicer updates, treat migrated profiles as needing smoke verification before production claims.
