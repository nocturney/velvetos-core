# Transcription and ASR verification

- Preserve the original audio and language context.
- Use VAD/segmentation appropriate to the material and keep timestamps when captions/edit sync are needed.
- Review names, numbers, dates, product terms and low-confidence/unclear passages manually or against source evidence.
- Do not silently normalize factual wording if the task requires a verbatim transcript.
- For captions, separate transcription correctness from line-breaking/timing/readability.
- A TTS back-transcription mismatch is a diagnostic signal, not proof by itself; listen to the audio around the mismatch.
