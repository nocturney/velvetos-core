# Print color and output QA

Use when color-management or ink behavior can affect a physical print deliverable.

- Resolve the provider's output condition/profile and spot/process policy before converting or flattening color.
- Keep document working spaces, placed-asset profiles and final output intent distinguishable; do not convert merely to make every object report the same color space.
- Inspect separations in the exact exported artifact, including unexpected spot inks, rich/registration black misuse and objects affected by overprint.
- Check total ink coverage against the provider/profile requirement rather than a universal TAC number.
- Confirm transparency flattening/rasterization behavior only when the target PDF/provider requires it.
- Inspect small text/line art and reversed elements at print size for fill/registration risk.
- Keep neutral grays and brand/spot colors tied to the agreed proof/output condition.
- For proof-critical work, record whether the review is soft proof, contract proof or provider proof; a monitor preview is not press evidence.
- Re-open the PDF/package and verify output intent, page boxes, separations and embedded/outlined font state according to the provider specification.

Do not claim press match without the appropriate calibrated/proofing evidence.
