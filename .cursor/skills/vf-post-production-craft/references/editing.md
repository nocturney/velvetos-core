# Editorial craft

- Start from the message, proof and intended audience action. Build beats before decorating cuts.
- Keep only shots/sections that advance information, emotion, proof or rhythm. For each proposed cut, identify the primary reason: emotional beat, story/information, rhythm, eye trace, 2D composition or 3D continuity.
- Prefer clean cuts when they communicate better than a transition. Use transitions to express a real relation in time, space or form.
- Protect continuity of action, gaze, screen direction and important object position unless a deliberate break serves the piece.
- Use J/L-style audio continuity when it improves flow; avoid using it mechanically.
- For short-form content, front-load clarity and proof without turning every opening into clickbait.
- Preserve source synchronization and timebase. Mixed frame rates or variable-frame-rate sources must be resolved deliberately before precision timing.
- Review the sequence without effects/grade when needed to expose weak structure, and again at real playback speed to judge holds, reactions and information density.
- After picture lock, re-check titles, overlays, captions and cut points on the final aspect ratio/mobile framing when relevant.
