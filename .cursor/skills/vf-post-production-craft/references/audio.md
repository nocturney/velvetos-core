# Audio post craft

- Preserve intelligibility before loudness or polish.
- Diagnose noise, hum, clicks, clipping, room tone and spectral imbalance separately; apply the least destructive repair that solves the audible problem.
- Avoid aggressive denoise that creates pumping, chirping or metallic artifacts.
- Use EQ/dynamics to support clarity, not to force every voice into one preset.
- Keep dialogue, music and effects balanced according to narrative priority.
- Preserve useful room tone across edits when silence changes become distracting.
- Resolve loudness/peak targets from the destination/platform/broadcaster requirement; do not hardcode a single universal target.
- Listen to the rendered master, not only the timeline mix.
