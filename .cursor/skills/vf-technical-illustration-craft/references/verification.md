# Technical illustration verification

1. Confirm source file/model and revision.
2. Compare silhouette, orientation and exploded relationships with the source.
3. Validate every callout/balloon target and identifier.
4. Validate all displayed dimensions/units against source geometry or approved engineering data.
5. Check text hierarchy, leader crossings, occlusion and readability at delivery size.
6. Export and inspect the exact PDF/vector/raster deliverable.
