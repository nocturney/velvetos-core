# Velvet Technical Illustration Craft

Turn verified engineering/product sources into clear technical communication without changing the source-of-truth geometry or part identity. For a dimensioned manufacturing PDF or another fabrication artifact, the mandatory Fabrication Router and its `engineering-drawing`/other selected upstream skill remain the execution authority; this skill supplies communication craft and QA only.

## Workflow

1. Resolve audience and purpose: assembly, service, parts catalog, instruction, dimensional drawing or explanatory graphic.
2. Read `projection-exploded.md`, `callouts-dimensions.md`, `technical-drawing-systems.md`, `parts-data.md`, `technical-publication.md`, `assembly-graphics.md`, `verification.md` and `tool-routing.md` as needed.
3. Keep source model/revision identity attached to the illustration job.
4. Use projected views, exploded spacing and callout hierarchy to reveal relationships, not decorate them.
5. Reconcile labels/balloons/dimensions against the source before export.

## Hard stops

- Do not alter engineering dimensions in the illustration to improve visual fit.
- Do not invent part numbers, torque values, steps or safety instructions.
- Do not detach callouts/dimensions from source relationships unless the reason is recorded.
- Do not present a perspective illustration as a scaled manufacturing drawing.

## Verification and completion contract

Before completion, verify the exact exported artifact by reopening or independently inspecting it and reconcile its source revision, projected/exploded relationships, part IDs, callout targets and displayed dimensions against the authoritative engineering/product source. A successful export alone is not verification.

Report `source_revision_known`, `view_projection_checked`, `parts_mapping_checked`, `callouts_checked`, `dimensions_checked`, `export_checked` separately.
