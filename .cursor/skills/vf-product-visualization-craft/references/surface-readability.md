# Surface readability

- Review silhouette, major planes, curvature transitions, seams, bevels, labels and material boundaries separately.
- A beauty render must not hide a protected defect with darkness, depth of field, glare or reflection.
- Inspect a neutral diagnostic plus the beauty frame when geometry/material correctness matters.
- Check normals, faceting, pinching, texture stretch, roughness discontinuities and edge ringing at stress highlights.
- Preserve material truth from `vf-material-lookdev`; presentation may reveal or obscure response but must not redefine it.
- Compare high-gloss and diffuse areas for coherent scale and micro-surface behavior.
- At reduced size, confirm that the intended primary form still reads before decorative detail.
