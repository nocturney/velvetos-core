# Product visualization QA

- Review the neutral diagnostic before judging the beauty render.
- Inspect intended viewing size, full-resolution detail and at least one reduced/thumbnail view.
- Compare protected silhouette, ports/interfaces, labels, material identity and colorway against source.
- Critique camera/perspective, light hierarchy, reflection shape, surface readability, contact/shadow and set distraction separately.
- Record the worst/stress angle on a turntable rather than approving from the best frame.
- Re-open the exported master and verify dimensions/frame count/color metadata when relevant.
- A successful render proves file production only; it does not prove source fidelity, beauty, commercial approval or publication authority.
