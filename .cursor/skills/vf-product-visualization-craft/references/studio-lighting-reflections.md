# Studio lighting and reflection shaping

- Light the product by controlling visible gradients and reflection geometry on its surfaces.
- Separate illumination from reflection design: a bright source may illuminate well but create a poor highlight shape.
- Use large sources, flags, negative fill and controlled rims to describe curvature, edge breaks and material transitions.
- Prefer one readable light hierarchy over many equal sources.
- Treat reflection cards/flags as scene objects with traceable purpose; remove any that only add noise.
- Validate chrome/glossy/dark materials under neutral conditions before beauty polish.
- Watch for highlights that hide seams, buttons, ports, logos or defects.
- Match shadow direction, softness and contact behavior to the implied physical set.
