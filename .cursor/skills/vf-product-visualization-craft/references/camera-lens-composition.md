# Camera, lens and composition

- Choose camera position and lens from the product-reading goal, not from a fashionable focal length.
- Preserve believable perspective and proportional truth; extreme wide/tele compression is a creative effect, not a neutral view.
- Use orthographic or long-lens-like inspection only when it serves dimensional/form comparison; label non-perspective presentation when needed.
- Set camera height and azimuth to expose the product's primary silhouette and functional face without hiding critical interfaces.
- Prefer deliberate edge separation and negative space over arbitrary three-quarter rotation.
- Check hero composition at intended crop plus a reduced/thumbnail view.
- When multiple variants share a campaign, lock the camera relationship unless the variation itself requires a new view.
