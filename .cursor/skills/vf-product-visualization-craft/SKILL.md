# Velvet Product Visualization Craft

Own the presentation layer for an already-authoritative product/model/material source. Consume geometry from CAD/DCC authority and material response from `vf-material-lookdev`; never silently change either to make the picture easier.

## Workflow

1. Lock the protected product truth: source revision, silhouette, dimensions/interfaces, material identity, logos/labels and required colorway.
2. Read only the needed references: `shot-system.md`, `camera-lens-composition.md`, `studio-lighting-reflections.md`, `surface-readability.md`, `set-styling-continuity.md`, `qa.md`, `tool-routing.md`, `provenance.md`.
3. Define the shot purpose and acceptance evidence before lighting: diagnostic, hero, catalog, detail, turntable or campaign variant.
4. Create a neutral diagnostic state before the beauty state so presentation cannot hide geometry, material or shading defects.
5. Build camera, lens, lighting and reflection structure around the product's form hierarchy.
6. Critique the exact render: observation -> likely cause -> corrective action -> verification.
7. Re-open/read back the final output and reconcile it with the protected source.

## Core rules

- Camera perspective must support truthful shape reading; do not use focal-length or perspective distortion to fake product proportions.
- Treat reflections as form-description tools. Shape and place them deliberately rather than adding lights until the frame looks busy.
- Separate neutral material validation from beauty lighting. A flattering highlight is not proof of correct roughness, normals or geometry.
- Keep set dressing subordinate to product hierarchy and avoid props that imply unverified size, compatibility, usage or included accessories.
- Maintain shot-to-shot camera, lighting, material, label and colorway continuity when variants belong to one family.
- For synthetic/public imagery, preserve existing product-truth and review/publish gates.

## Hard stops

- Do not modify protected CAD dimensions, mesh interfaces, material identity or brand assets to improve a render.
- Do not invent ports, buttons, labels, accessories, package contents or scale cues.
- Do not replace a verified product image/source with a synthetic lookalike while representing it as the real item.
- Do not add a new renderer, cloud service, model download or arbitrary scripting surface from this Skill.
- Do not treat a render completion receipt as publication authorization.

## Completion contract

Report `source_locked`, `shot_plan_defined`, `neutral_diagnostic_checked`, `camera_checked`, `lighting_reflections_checked`, `surface_readability_checked`, `beauty_checked`, and `final_output_verified` separately.
