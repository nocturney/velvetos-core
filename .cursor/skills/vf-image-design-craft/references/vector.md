# Vector craft

- Build geometry from intentional paths, anchors, fills, strokes and reusable symbols/components rather than excess points.
- Keep curves smooth with the fewest control points that express the intended shape.
- Preserve live text and editable appearances until outlining/expanding is required for delivery or compatibility.
- Check compound paths, clipping masks, stroke alignment, joins/caps and expanded effects before export to constrained formats.
- Keep units, artboard/page bounds and scale explicit for technical or print-adjacent artwork.
- Use the exact approved brand asset instead of tracing/recreating a logo unless a deliberate redraw project is authorized.
- Re-open SVG/PDF/EPS or other vector exports in a second view when interoperability matters.
