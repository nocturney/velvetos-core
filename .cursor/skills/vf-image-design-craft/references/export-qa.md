# Image/vector export QA

1. Confirm exact pixel/page/artboard dimensions and orientation.
2. Confirm destination color/profile handling and transparency/alpha requirements.
3. Check text for spelling, RTL order, missing glyphs and unintended font substitution.
4. Inspect crops, edges, masks, gradients, shadows and thin strokes at 100% and intended display size.
5. Re-open the exact exported file; do not sign off from the source document alone.
6. For vector delivery, confirm paths/text/effects survived export as intended.
7. For raster delivery, check resampling, sharpening, banding, compression and alpha fringes.
8. Keep editable master and delivery derivative as separate artifacts.
