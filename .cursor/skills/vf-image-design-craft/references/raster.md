# Raster editing and compositing

- Preserve an untouched or recoverable source before destructive retouching.
- Prefer masks and adjustment layers to irreversible pixel changes when editability has value.
- Correct exposure, white balance, lens/perspective issues and obvious defects before adding stylistic treatment.
- When compositing, match edge softness, perspective, lighting direction, contrast, grain/noise and depth cues.
- Inspect masks against contrasting backgrounds to expose halos, contamination and missed edges.
- Distinguish cleanup of real detail from fabrication of missing evidence-bearing detail.
- Resample only when the destination requires it; compare the resampled export against the source for sharpening/ringing or invented detail.
- Keep product geometry and identifying features source-locked when publication truth depends on them.
