# Composition and typography

- Establish one clear visual hierarchy: primary subject/message, secondary information, then supporting detail.
- Use alignment, spacing, grouping and contrast to express relationships before adding decoration.
- Protect important subject areas, crop boundaries and platform/print safe zones.
- Evaluate typography for legibility, line length, weight, spacing and hierarchy at intended viewing size.
- Treat Hebrew/RTL as real bidirectional typography: preserve word order, punctuation, numerals and alignment in the final rendered artifact.
- Prefer deliberate whitespace over filling every region.
- Keep brand styling consistent with the current approved brand authority; this skill does not invent a new visual identity.
