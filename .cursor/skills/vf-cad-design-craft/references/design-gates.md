# Product design gates and evidence discipline

Use these gates when inventing or materially changing a product rather than only editing known geometry.

- Lock a short design DNA: the few product traits or innovations that revisions must preserve unless the owner explicitly changes direction.
- Separate observation, inference, assumption and requirement. Unsupported critical geometry stays TBD rather than becoming a convenient dimension.
- Treat reference authority as field-specific. A brand page may establish appearance but not hidden connector geometry, tolerances or internal construction.
- Use maturity gates: brief -> concept/massing -> form language -> interfaces/ergonomics -> surface quality -> CMF -> engineering/DFM -> handoff.
- A failed gate returns to the stage that owns the defect. More detail or a polished render cannot compensate for unresolved form, interface or mechanical logic.
- Compare concepts at consistent scale/camera conditions so presentation bias does not choose the winner.
- Preserve regression views or measurements for locked traits: silhouette, key interfaces, motion states, proportions and protected clearances.
- Keep revision identity aligned across sketches, CAD, renders, technical views and BOM-linked derivatives.
- Record rejected directions and why they failed so later iterations do not silently repeat them.

Never import a foreign standard as authority merely because a source Skill uses it. Standards, safety and manufacturing requirements must come from the actual project authority.
