# Ergonomics and CMF boundary

- Define user population, task, posture, reach/clearance need, duration, PPE/clothing and environment before applying anthropometric data.
- A percentile has no universal meaning without naming the population, measure, sex/age grouping where applicable, dataset/date and design task.
- Use current public human-system references such as NASA guidance and DINED as scoped evidence sources; do not embed unverified tables as permanent defaults.
- Design for accommodation of the relevant user range rather than choosing a familiar percentile by habit.
- Separate ergonomic geometry from CMF. Color/material/finish may improve grip cues, hierarchy or perception but cannot repair a dimensionally inaccessible design.
- Treat perceived quality, gloss, texture, seam placement and touch surfaces as product-design variables that must not violate material/process/safety constraints.
- Document which ergonomic decisions are evidence-backed, which are prototypes/tests, and which remain unresolved.
