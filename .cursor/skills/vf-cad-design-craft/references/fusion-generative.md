# Fusion generative and constraint-driven exploration

Use when form should emerge from real engineering constraints, loads, keep-outs or manufacturing requirements rather than from styling alone.

- Define preserve geometry, obstacle/keep-out geometry, attachment interfaces and load/constraint cases before generating outcomes.
- Use only load values, materials and boundary conditions supported by engineering evidence. Unknown loads stay unknown; do not invent them to make a study run.
- Select manufacturing constraints that match the intended process and orientation when they are part of the real brief.
- Generate multiple outcomes and compare them on functional metrics first: mass, stiffness/response, interface preservation, accessibility and manufacturability.
- Treat visual novelty as a secondary selection dimension, not proof that an outcome is better.
- Reject outcomes that create inaccessible fasteners, fragile unsupported members, impossible clearances or interfaces that drift from protected geometry.
- Preserve the study setup and selected outcome as evidence; record why one family was chosen over another.
- Refine or reconstruct the chosen result into an editable production master when downstream change control requires it.
- Re-run dimensional, DfAM/DFM and assembly checks after reconstruction or aesthetic refinement.

Generative output is design exploration, not structural certification. Final engineering claims require the appropriate validated analysis and human/engineering authority.
