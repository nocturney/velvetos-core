# Product material lookdev handoff

Use this reference when material response must be proven before product presentation begins. Camera, hero lighting, reflection shaping, set styling and shot-family ownership now belong to `vf-product-visualization-craft`.

- Start with a neutral/clay or otherwise controlled diagnostic so form, normals, bevels and scale can be judged without beauty-light distraction.
- Add material lobes and texture channels progressively; change one major variable at a time.
- Keep physical scale explicit because roughness breakup, bump, displacement, SSS and transmission are scale-sensitive.
- Judge authored materials under a neutral rig and at least one alternate condition so the response is not dependent on one flattering environment.
- Inspect high-contrast reflections only as material/surface diagnostics; the final reflection design belongs to Product Visualization.
- Use subtle imperfection only where reference/product truth supports it; dust, fingerprints and scratches are not mandatory realism.
- Verify exact exported textures/materials and their re-import/readback before handoff.
- Handoff must identify the authoritative material set, color/normal conventions, unresolved surface issues and any lighting conditions that exposed defects.
