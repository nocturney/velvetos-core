## Rule

- Veto term (regex below) on a line.
- `EVIDENCE_BACKED_BLOCKER`: the same line also has a **blocker term** and a **citation**. Blocker terms: security/secret/credential, licence, copyright, rights, archived, deprecated, unmaintained, cost, paid, subscription, GPU, hardware, OS-only, privacy, PII, non-commercial, terms of service, and `curl | bash`. Citations: a URL, an ISO date, `#issue`, or `archived upstream`.
- `EVIDENCE_FREE_VETO`: everything else. It needs a fair comparison before its disposition stands; it is not a verdict to drop.
- `blocker_term_without_citation`: a blocker term is present but no citation is on the line. These need a citation or a rewrite before they can be classed as blockers.
- Bare `"skip"` and `"verdict": "skip"` lines carry no reason, so they are included as EVIDENCE_FREE_VETO rows (14 rows).
- Matches inside `title`, `pattern`, `id`, `rule`, `item`, `kind` and `url` values are skipped as descriptions, not dispositions.
- Item = the same-line `name`/`id`/`title`, otherwise the nearest preceding one. Candidate IDs come from the alias map (URL or exact label match only).
